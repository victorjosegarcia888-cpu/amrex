# Regenerative Cooling

## Principle

Regenerative cooling uses the propellant (CH4) as coolant before it enters the combustion chamber.

## Flow Path

1. CH4 enters at 20°C, 450 bar
2. Flows through helical channels in chamber wall
3. Absorbs heat, exits at ~750°C
4. Enters injectors as hot gas

## Heat Transfer

### Bartz Correlation

hg = 0.026 * mu^0.2 * cp^0.6 * (Pc/C*)^0.8 * Dt^0.2 * Rc^-0.1 * (At/A)^0.9 / Pr^0.6

### Channel Geometry

- Width: 2-6 mm
- Height: 1-2 mm
- Pitch: 2-3x channel width
- Number: 80-120 channels

## Nusselt Number

Nu = hg * Dh / k

Internal flow Nusselt numbers for turbulent regime: 100-1000

## Pressure Drop

DeltaP = f * (L/Dh) * (rho*V^2/2)

where f = friction factor (Blasius: f = 0.316/Re^0.25)
