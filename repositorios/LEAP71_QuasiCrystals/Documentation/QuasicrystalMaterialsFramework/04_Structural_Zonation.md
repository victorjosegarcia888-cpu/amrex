# 4. Structural zonation for engine and capsule

## Nozzle throat

In the most severe zone of the engine, the structure must withstand internal pressure, thermal flow, and aggressive oxidation. The 3D preform must be very dense and with a suitable fiber orientation to absorb circumferential stresses.

## Inlet/outlet transition

In these regions, the strategy can change. A greater controlled porosity can be introduced to slow down heat conduction and reduce heat transmission to neighboring components.

## Re-entry shield

The front exposed to plasma needs a surface with high capacity to radiate heat. An outer skin rich in protective phase can be designed, with a gradual transition toward a lighter and more porous core.

## Conceptual advantage

Zonation allows not manufacturing a uniform part, but a component whose properties change in a controlled way along the volume. This fits very well with the idea of generative design based on voxels or implicit fields.
